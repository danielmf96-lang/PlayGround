HOW TO DIVE

01

SWIM & SEARCH

Use WASD / arrows or the touch stick. Deeper salvage pays more. Your lamp only reaches so far.

02

MANAGE AIR

Air burns faster with depth and speed. Grab teal canisters. At 0 you black out.

03

Bottom time loads nitrogen. The amber ceiling is the shallowest depth you can safely reach. Hold at it to off-gas.

04

The ceiling rises as you off-gas. Follow it up. Cross above it and the bends meter fills. Full = dead.
